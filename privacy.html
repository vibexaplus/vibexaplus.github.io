Summary in plain words

  • Vibexa Plus has no account or sign-in, and it contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • Nothing is sent to the developer. Your videos, music, downloads and settings stay on your phone.
  • The app only goes online to download something you asked for, and to keep its download engine up to date from GitHub.
  • This website, separate from the app, counts page views and download-button clicks with GoatCounter. The query string is left out, and a browser privacy opt-out stops the count. Details are under “This website”.

What stays on your phone

The app reads the videos and music on your phone so it can show and play them. It saves your download list, play positions and settings in its own storage, and saves finished downloads to your phone’s media folders. All of this stays on your device. The app’s own data is removed when you uninstall it; files saved to your media folders stay until you delete them.

What the developer receives

Nothing. The app has no server of its own, so the developer doesn’t receive your name, email, location, contacts, what you watch or what you download.

When the app uses the internet

  • Downloads you start: when you paste a link, your phone connects directly to that website. When you open a magnet link or torrent, your phone connects to other people sharing it, who can see your IP address; the app stops sharing once the download finishes. These sites and peers handle your data under their own policies.
  • Download engine updates: at most once a week, only on Wi‑Fi or another unmetered connection, and never during a download, the app checks GitHub for a newer version of its download engine (yt-dlp) and fetches it. You can also start this from Settings. GitHub sees your IP address, as with any web request.

Only download things you own or have permission to save.

Permissions and why

  • Videos and music (media access; storage on older Android versions): to find and play your media. On Android 8 and 9, storage access is also used to save finished downloads.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls and download progress.
  • Internet and network state: to download what you asked for and to update the download engine, and to wait for a connection or Wi‑Fi.
  • Foreground service and wake lock: to keep music playing and downloads running while the screen is off.

This website

The notes above are about the Vibexa Plus app. This section is only about vibexaplus.github.io. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the app file is served from GitHub Releases. GitHub may log visitors’ IP addresses for security and operations; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

This website counts page views, and clicks on the Vibexa Plus download button, with GoatCounter. The browser loads GoatCounter’s count script and sends counts to https://vibexaplus.goatcounter.com/count. If that script is blocked or fails, the page still opens and the download still starts.

The page identity is the path only, for example / or /privacy.html. The query string and fragment, anything after ? or #, are removed before a count is sent, including from the referrer. Opening a page does not count as a download. The download count is a separate event named download-vibexa-plus, and it is sent only when you activate the download button with a click or keyboard action that actually follows the link. That event is not GitHub’s release asset download count (download_count). GitHub’s figure is the download requests recorded for the GitHub release asset. It is not a count of unique people, confirmed completed transfers, installs, or website clicks. A download request can be recorded without a click here, and a click here is not itself that request.

The count does not use a cookie, a localStorage identifier, or session replay. GoatCounter’s script does send the screen width, the page title, and the usual browser headers, such as User-Agent and language. GoatCounter’s privacy documentation says it stores aggregate counts (pages, browsers, systems, languages, screen widths, and country) in separate tables, and that it does not store the IP address or the full User-Agent. It also says it keeps the site, IP address, and User-Agent in memory for up to eight hours so a repeat visit is not counted again. This page is describing that documentation, not promising a legal result.

You can turn these counts off in this browser. The site does not load the count script when Global Privacy Control is on, when the browser sends Do Not Track (1 or yes), or when GoatCounter’s own opt-out is set. To use that opt-out, open a page on this site with #toggle-goatcounter at the end of the address, and open a page that way again to turn counting back on. Counting follows the new choice on the next page opened without that ending. The opt-out stores an on/off flag in localStorage under the key skipgc. That flag is not an identifier. GoatCounter’s note on consent is their explanation, not a decision that this website meets any particular law.

This website does not show a public visitor counter. The GoatCounter dashboard is for logged-in users only, and GoatCounter’s optional individual pageview rows are intended to stay off. Those are account settings, separate from the app.

Children

Vibexa Plus isn’t directed at children under 13. The app doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
